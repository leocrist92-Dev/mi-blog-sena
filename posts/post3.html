Job Application Request

Introduction: Writing formal emails is a fundamental soft skill in the software industry. Below is an example of a formal job application letter created during our English classes.

Documento escrito
Aprendices: Leonardo Silva, Anyelina Guadalupe Huertas
SENA-Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje
Análisis y Desarrollo de Software
9/04/2026

Subject: Job Application Request for Available Position - Apprentice Software Developer

Dear Sir or Madam

Receive a respectful and cordial greeting. I hope you are doing very well and that your daily activities are developing successfully. My name is Noah Giraldo, and I am currently an apprentice in software development. It is a pleasure to contact you through this email, expressing my respect for your organization and the work you do, as well as my interest in communicating in a formal way as part of my learning process.

I am writing this email as a formal job application solicitud for the position of Apprentice Software Developer in your organization. I am currently in training as a software development apprentice, and I am highly motivated to continue strengthening my technical and professional skills in a company that values learning, collaboration and quality in its projects.

In this role, I am particularly interested in contributing to the development and maintenance of software solutions while gaining experience in real work environments. Throughout my training process, I have acquired basic knowledge in programming, databases and version control, as well as experience working on academic projects where I have learned to analyze requirements, propose solutions and work in a team to meet objectives and deadlines. I consider myself a responsible, committed and adaptable person, with good communication skills and a strong desire to keep learning from more experienced professionals.

I would be honored to bring my enthusiasm, my willingness to learn and my growing technical abilities to your company, always respecting institutional policies, professional ethics and the guidelines for digital communication and netiquette. If you consider my profile suitable for the position, I would greatly appreciate the opportunity to participate in the selection process. You can contact me using the phone number and email address indicated in my CV and at the end of this message, at the time most convenient for you.

I would like to thank you in advance for your time and attention to this request. I remain attentive to any additional information you may require and fully available to continue in the corresponding process.

Kind regards
Noah Giraldo
Medellín
Apprentice Software Developer

In this letter, I emphasize my basic knowledge in programming and databases, and my commitment to institutional policies and professional ethics.
