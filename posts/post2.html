Basilica of the Lord of Miracles

Introduction: In this entry, we present a video about a highly significant cultural and religious place in Colombia. Practicing oral presentation skills is a key part of our training.

The Basilica of the Lord of Miracles is located in Buga, Valle del Cauca. Its history dates back to the 16th century when a woman found a small crucifix in the river. The architecture is neoclassical, featuring high ceilings and beautiful stained glass windows. It is one of the most visited pilgrimage destinations in Colombia, representing deep faith and tradition.
